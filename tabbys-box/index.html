Help and contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email friendlybearstudio@gmail.com. Please include your device (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad Air) and what happened.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Scores, unlocked looks and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes them.

Does the game need the internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Can I change the language? Yes: Settings (the cushion in the menu room) → Language.
