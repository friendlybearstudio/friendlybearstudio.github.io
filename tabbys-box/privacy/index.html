Tabby's Box Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

In short: The current version of Tabby's Box does not collect personal information through gameplay. Your progress and settings are saved locally on your device.

Who we are

Tabby's Box is made by Jordan, an independent developer working as Friendly Bear Studio ("we"). You can reach us at friendlybearstudio@gmail.com.

Information collected through the game

None. The current version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no tracking. The game does not connect to the internet and does not send your information to us or to anyone else. The only link out of the game is the link to this page in Settings, which opens in your browser.

Information kept on your device

To work, the game saves a few things locally on your iPhone or iPad: your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, dark mode), your scores and bests, your Challenge progress, the game in progress, and the looks you have unlocked. This information is not sent anywhere by the game. Deleting the app removes these local saves from your device. Copies may remain in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud Backup), which are managed by you and Apple, not by us.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

If you email us

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use them only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We keep support emails only as long as needed for that, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Children

Tabby's Box does not collect personal information through gameplay, from children or from anyone else.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement. That is separate from the game, which collects nothing.

Future versions and changes to this policy

The current version contains no advertisements or in-app purchases. Future versions may introduce banner advertisements and optional cosmetic purchases. Before introducing these features, we will update this policy and our App Store privacy disclosures to explain any changes to data handling and the privacy choices available to you. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: friendlybearstudio@gmail.com.
